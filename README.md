## 🏛️ Company Heritage & Executive Leadership

### The Journey from Movie Source to FamilyFlix (2023–2026)
FamilyFlix was originally established in **2023** under the founding name **Movie Source**. The company was born out of a visionary initiative to digitally archive, index, verify, and stream public-domain motion pictures, historic television series, and Creative Commons cinema.

In **2026**, the platform underwent a comprehensive corporate transformation:
1. **Rebranding:** Formally transitioned from *Movie Source* to **FamilyFlix**.
2. **DemureGram Strategic Alliance:** Partnered with **DemureGram**, expanding the platform into an end-to-end entertainment network, artificial intelligence ecosystem, and developer API platform.

### Leadership Transition & The DemureGram Foundation
The company was originally founded and guided by **Princeton T. Taylor**. Following his disability in 2026, Princeton T. Taylor made the decisive leadership transition to turn the entire enterprise, business operations, intellectual property, and foundation assets over to **Prisandra T. Taylor**.

**Prisandra T. Taylor** now serves as the **Founder & CEO** over the **DemureGram Foundation** and the FamilyFlix enterprise. Under her leadership, FamilyFlix has expanded into a cloud-native, autonomous streaming ecosystem, scaling its catalog to over 23,270 titles, deploying dedicated family and kids safe modes, and creating commercial opportunities for independent developers.

## 💻 For Developers — API Platform & Subscriptions

The **FamilyFlix Developer Platform By DemureGram** provides high-performance, developer-friendly REST APIs for building streaming apps, movie recommender engines, cinema databases, and academic archives.

### Developer API Endpoints
All developer requests are secured through the **FamilyFlix API Gateway** (`/v1`):
- `GET /v1/titles`: Search, paginate, and filter 23,270+ titles with rich metadata.
- `GET /v1/titles/:slug`: Detailed title metadata, runtime, director, cast, and video assets.
- `GET /v1/categories`: Live title counts across all 44 main genres and 20 subgenres.
- `GET /v1/categories/:slug`: Query titles belonging to a specific category with year and content filters.
- `GET /v1/streaming/:slug`: Retrieve verified HLS streaming manifests and audio tracks.
- `POST /v1/tts/synthesize`: Text-to-speech audio synthesis for title narration.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack & Cloud Infrastructure

- **Frontend Application:** React 19, TypeScript, Vite 7, TailwindCSS, Lucide Icons, Wouter routing.
- **Backend API Server:** Node.js 22, Express 5, Pino logging, Standalone esbuild bundle.
- **Database:** PostgreSQL 16 on Railway with persistent volume `/var/lib/postgresql/data`, Drizzle ORM.
- **Cloud Hosting:** Railway VM replicas (SFO region) with zero local PC dependency.
- **Edge Network & Security:** Cloudflare WAF, Full (Strict) SSL/TLS, edge caching, DDoS mitigation.
- **Media Engine:** HLS streaming, MP4 byte-range delivery, WebVTT subtitle tracks.
